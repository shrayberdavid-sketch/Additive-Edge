The opportunity

Say yes to the jobs you're turning away

Every shop has them. A part with internal channels no tool can reach. A lattice bracket. A small run that would tie up a machine for a week. Or a good job you just can't schedule right now.

How it works

From your customer's RFQ to delivered parts

01

Send the job

Send us the part files, drawings and material data sheets for your customer's job.

02

Approve the cost

We recommend a process and material and send you our cost. Nothing starts until you approve it, and you set your own price with your customer.

03

We print & ship

Once you approve, we print the parts and ship them to you. You get the invoice when they ship.

04

You QA & deliver

Check the parts against your customer's spec when they arrive, then deliver them.

Common questions

See all 69 answers in the FAQ →

How much does it cost a machine shop to join?

Nothing. There is no partnership fee and no minimum.

How does pricing work for partners?

We send you our cost before any work starts, and nothing begins until you approve it. You set your own price with your customer.

Will my customer know Additive Edge made the part?

No. You keep the customer relationship and deliver the parts under your name.

What kinds of jobs do machine shops send to Additive Edge?

Geometries that can't be machined, such as internal channels, lattices, organic shapes and consolidated assemblies. Also overflow work during a long backlog, and materials a shop doesn't run, such as high-performance polymers, composites, ceramics and printed metals.

Can partners get an NDA?

Yes. We sign an NDA on request.
