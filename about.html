About

A decade in additive,
on both sides

We've spent over a decade in 3D printing - on the customer side and the OEM side. Working with suppliers, resellers, and service providers, we kept seeing the same thing: no one is an expert at every technology, and there are far too many printers and materials to navigate alone. We built Additive Edge to fix that.

Who we are

One source for every technology

After years on both sides of additive manufacturing, we learned that great parts come from matching the right process and material to the job - not from forcing every part through one machine. So we created Additive Edge to give customers access to every technology and material through a single partnership.

Many of the most capable 3D printing technologies are still out of reach - costly to acquire, demanding to maintain, and steep to learn. Our expertise and partnerships bridge that gap, getting customers the parts they need regardless of the system.

Our ultimate goal is to bring as many of these technologies in-house as possible - a future built by the customers who keep working with us and believe in our mission to make 3D printing more accessible. Based in Sharon, Massachusetts, we serve customers worldwide through our partner network.
