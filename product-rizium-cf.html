Key benefits

Why print in Rizium CF

Stable at negative temperatures

Holds its mechanical properties in sub-zero conditions, so parts stay reliable in cold environments.

Lighter weight

Lighter than standard FDM materials, so parts weigh less without giving up performance.

Nylon-like, far easier to print

Similar properties to Nylon and comparable composites, but much easier to print, with no warping and no need to dehydrate the material.
