Specs

General material characteristics

These categories summarize the core properties we use to match materials to your part's application.

Polymers
Good surface finish, chemical resistance, and impact strength; ideal for functional prototypes, jigs, fixtures and end-use parts.
Composites
Enhanced stiffness and strength with reduced warping and thermal expansion; suited for structural components and lightweight assemblies.
Ceramics
High-temperature stability, abrasion resistance and electrical insulation; used for tooling, wear parts and precision electrical components.
Metals
Strong, heat-resistant and durable; supporting load-bearing parts, fixtures, housings and metal prototypes with alloys like Titanium, 316L, 17-4, H13, Copper and Inconel 718.
Specialty prints
Custom-engineered materials for flexibility, castability, conductivity, biocompatibility and other advanced functional requirements.
