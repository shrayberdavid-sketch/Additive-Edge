How It Works

From file to finished part

01

Send your part

Describe your part and share critical details; we will reach out to schedule a review meeting and align design, fit, and production intent.

02

We suggest the optimal process, material and machine in-house or through our vetted manufacturing network.

03

Parts are printed, finished and inspected to engineering-grade standards.

04

Finished, ready-to-use parts arrive at your door. One vendor, one invoice. Lead times run from 2 business days to about 4 weeks, depending on material and quantity. We confirm your date with the quote.
