Privacy policy

What information Additive Edge collects through this website, why we collect it and what we do with it.

Last updated November 15, 2026

Who we are

Additive Edge is a 3D printing service based in Sharon, MA 02067, USA. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Additive Edge. If you have a question about your information, email david@additive-edge.com or call 617-331-6266.

What we collect

Quote and partner program forms. When you send a form, we receive your name, company, email address and phone number, plus the project details you choose to include, such as a part description, material, quantity, timeline, tolerances and links to your files. The forms are delivered to us by email through a form service called Web3Forms.

Email and phone. If you contact us directly, we keep what you send us so we can reply and follow up.

Shop orders. Our online shop runs on Shopify. Checkout and payment happen on Shopify's secure pages, and we never see or store your full card number. We receive the order details we need to ship it, such as your name, email address, shipping address and the items you bought.

Website analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site: which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are (city or country), the device and browser used, how they found us, and actions such as opening the cart, starting checkout or sending a form. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. We don't use it to show you ads.

Your cart. Your browser stores a cart ID so items stay in your cart if you reload the page or come back later. It doesn't contain any personal information.

How we use it

  • To reply to your questions and prepare quotes
  • To produce, inspect and deliver your parts
  • To process and ship shop orders
  • To run the machine shop partner program
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Manufacturing partners. Some parts are produced by vetted partners in our network. When that happens, we share the files and specifications the partner needs to make your part. We are glad to sign an NDA before you share files.

Service providers. We use a small number of companies to run the site and the business: Web3Forms (form delivery), Shopify (online shop and checkout) and Google (analytics and website fonts). They handle information on our behalf under their own privacy terms.

When required by law. We may share information if the law requires it or to protect our rights.

Cookies and your choices

The only cookies on this site are from Google Analytics, plus whatever Shopify uses on its checkout pages. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site works without them.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests, project files and order records for as long as we need them to do the work, support you afterward and keep the business and tax records the law requires. Analytics data is kept according to our Google Analytics retention setting.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email david@additive-edge.com and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, and payments are handled entirely by Shopify. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so please share only the files and details we need for your project.

Children

This site is for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
